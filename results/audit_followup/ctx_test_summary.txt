=== CONCLUSION ===
Raising num_ctx from the (unset, auto-selected) default of 4096 to 8192 -- enough to hold the full
~6.5k-token 3-shot prompt without truncation -- RECOVERS Bot and Brute Force to perfect recall and
substantially recovers DoS/DDoS (0->0.70) and Infiltration (0->0.30). The previously reported "3-shot
collapse" (CLAUDE.md, Sep 11-12 entries) is overwhelmingly a context-window truncation artifact, not
(primarily) a "more few-shot examples confuses the model" effect. Every actual saved 3-shot-ablation run
(v3 and v4, all 6 runs across both dataset versions) used the untruncated default and is therefore
reporting a truncation artifact, not the intended ablation.

Benign recall also moves (0.10 -> 0.45) and Web Attack drops (1.00 -> 0.60) at num_ctx=8192 -- with full
context, Web Attack's dominance among predictions weakens (the model is no longer defaulting to
whichever example survived nearest the end of the truncated prompt) while a wider spread of classes gets
predicted, at the cost of some Web Attack recall.
